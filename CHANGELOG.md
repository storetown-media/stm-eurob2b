## [Unreleased]

## [1.4.0] – 2026-10-29

The first release as two plugins: **EuroB2B**, free and meant for the WordPress.org plugin directory, and the add-on **EuroB2B Pro** with the features of earlier releases that go beyond the core (both carry the same version; Pro requires EuroB2B). Shops that used Pro features (quotes, company accounts, order form, tier prices, invoice payment, tax reports …) install EuroB2B Pro so they stay available; nothing is lost while Pro is inactive (settings and data are kept, privacy export and erasure keep working). No database migration (database version stays 12). New external connection only in EuroB2B Pro and only after a licence key was entered: the licence and update service of Storetown Media.

### Added
- Licence and updates of EuroB2B Pro (PLT-016): the new screen EuroB2B → Licence activates a licence key from the Storetown Media shop for the site, shows status, edition (1, 3 or unlimited sites), the end of updates and support and the sites in use, checks on request and deactivates the site. Updates of EuroB2B Pro then arrive on the WordPress Plugins screen (header `Update URI`, so WordPress never asks WordPress.org for EuroB2B Pro); the signed download link is fetched when the update starts. New external connection to `www.storetown-media.de` (docs/privacy-and-data-flows.md §4, D-33): only the product, the licence key, the host name of the site and the plugin version are sent, as JSON over HTTPS, at most twice a day and never before a key was entered; the free plugin makes no such request. EuroB2B Pro keeps working with every licence state; only updates depend on it. The key is stored in an option that is not autoloaded, shown masked, and removed on deactivation and when EuroB2B Pro is deleted (new `pro/uninstall.php`, every site of a network). While EuroB2B Pro cannot start next to another version of EuroB2B, the licence screen (under Plugins) and the updater still work, so the matching update can be installed. The record of processing activities gets EB-15 and the privacy policy suggestion a section on the update connection while a key is stored. Contract with the shop's licence API: docs/licence-api.md. Developers: filter `eurob2b_licence_api_base`.
- `readme.txt` for the WordPress.org plugin directory (free edition): description of the free features, external services (VIES), FAQ, installation, upgrade notice for the split into EuroB2B and EuroB2B Pro. The plugin description in the header now describes the free edition.
- Audit file: orders EuroB2B treated as an export get a section *Export evidence* (TAX-031, TAX-032) after the proof of intra-Community supply, with status, type, MRN, date of exit, who recorded and accepted it, the documents by name and the retention date.
- Northern Ireland (TAX-034): with the new setting *Northern Ireland* (*Tax and VAT IDs*, off by default) goods to a United Kingdom address with a BT postcode are treated under EU VAT rules (§ 30 UStG, Windsor Framework): a business with a valid XI VAT ID gets the intra-Community supply under the same conditions as in other member states (without a reviewed country profile for XI the order is taxed and flagged), consumers and businesses with a GB VAT ID or none are taxed like sales to another member state, services stay under UK rules, and orders with goods and services are flagged for review. The recapitulative statement reports the goods with the XI VAT ID and leaves services to XI VAT IDs out; the proof of intra-Community supply covers these orders, the export evidence does not. While the setting is off, shop staff who manage the settings see a notice when recent orders or customers in Northern Ireland exist; while it is on, the plugin pages warn when no WooCommerce tax rate for the United Kingdom with the postcodes BT* exists. Off: nothing changes. No new personal data, no migration. Sources and review status in `docs/country-matrix.md` §2.1; recognition by the BT postcode rests on an indirect official source (ONS) and needs review.
- Free and Pro editions, part 1 (PLT-015): EuroB2B is built as two plugins from this repository. `bin/build-release.php` writes `eurob2b-<version>.zip` (free, without any Pro file and without dompdf) and `eurob2b-pro-<version>.zip` (EuroB2B Pro: company accounts, quotes and offers, order forms and purchase lists, conversations, coupons and currency per group, access control, e-invoicing data, reports and DATEV export, business stock, REST API). EuroB2B Pro requires EuroB2B of the same version (`Requires Plugins: eurob2b` and a check at runtime with an admin notice) and adds its modules in the order of the single plugin, so a shop that runs both plugins behaves as before. `bin/check-editions.php` (in `composer check` and CI) keeps free code free of Pro classes and paths; a CI job installs the free archive alone and runs its tests and a boot smoke test. Without EuroB2B Pro the import from B2BKing leaves out company accounts, visibility and quantity limits and says so. Part 2 splits the mixed modules (see below).
- Free and Pro editions, part 2 (PLT-015): the mixed modules are split, so the free archive contains no Pro code any more and is ready for WordPress.org. EuroB2B (free) keeps the price pipeline with base and group prices, percentage discount rules (audience, scope, priority, date window), payment and shipping method control per audience and customer, the VAT ID field with VIES checks, the validation records and the tax treatment and display. EuroB2B Pro adds tiers and the tier table, customer prices, price lists and the RRP; every other rule type, mode and condition and discount lines in the cart; the invoice, purchase order and offline gateways, purchase order numbers and documents, payment terms, payment windows, surcharges and discounts, free shipping rules and shipping costs per group, late payment and dunning; the BZSt, Swiss UID register and Brønnøysund checks, special territories, Northern Ireland, the exempt sales CSV, the recapitulative statement, the proof of supply, the export evidence, the OSS monitor and the audit file, with their settings, pages, e-mails and privacy texts. A shop that runs both plugins behaves as before.
- Privacy without EuroB2B Pro (product owner decision of 2026-10-29): the privacy export and erasure, WooCommerce's order anonymisation and the retention of the data EuroB2B Pro's features stored (companies and members, order approval, quotes and offers incl. guest quotes, conversations, purchase lists, e-invoicing data and Peppol lookup, DATEV debtor account, purchase order documents, dunning history, proof of supply, export evidence, customer prices) stay in EuroB2B and keep working when EuroB2B Pro is deactivated or deleted. Same exporters and erasers as before.

### Changed

EuroB2B Pro adds the differences of its features (order approval, company address, purchase order document, accepted offers, discount lines, quantity rules) through the new filter `eurob2b_blocks_differences`; with Pro the notice is unchanged.
- *Attach the offer as PDF* (EuroB2B Pro): when the PDF library is missing, the setting now says to install EuroB2B Pro from its release archive, which ships dompdf, instead of EuroB2B.
- Documentation for 1.4.0 (PLT-015): the user guides (`docs/guide/`) name for each chapter and feature whether it is part of EuroB2B or needs EuroB2B Pro; the setup guide starts with a section on the two editions, how EuroB2B Pro is installed and licensed; the README describes both plugins and their requirements. Stale passages corrected: the tax guide described Northern Ireland as planned and missed the export evidence and audit files, the privacy guide missed the Peppol Directory connection and several privacy policy sections, `docs/tax.md` said the audit-file section of the export evidence was not built.
- Privacy texts of the free edition (PLT-015): the record of processing activities (EB-05 order details, EB-10 audit log, special categories) and the suggested privacy policy text (business customer accounts) no longer name Pro features (order approval, purchase order number, company accounts, quote request forms, products agreed for a group). With EuroB2B Pro the texts are worded exactly as before, through the new filters `eurob2b_processing_record_special_categories` and `eurob2b_privacy_policy_account_text`.
- The recapitulative statement leaves services to customers with an XI VAT ID out of the file and lists them, because services to Northern Ireland are not reported (BZSt); this applies with or without the new setting.
- Without EuroB2B Pro, rules that use a part of EuroB2B Pro (another type, a fixed amount, a condition, a discount line in the cart) stay stored but do not apply; the rules page lists them without an edit link. The import from B2BKing leaves out customer prices, tier prices and such rules and says so.
- Saving the settings while EuroB2B Pro is inactive keeps the stored values of its settings (product owner decision of 2026-10-29); before, they were dropped like any unknown key.
- Deactivating EuroB2B Pro removes its scheduled event (OSS monitor refresh).
- The Pro settings of the sections *Tax and VAT IDs* and *Prices* are listed after the core settings of those sections.
- Developers: new hooks of the split (docs/architecture.md §2.1, docs/hooks.md): `eurob2b_rules_extended`, `eurob2b_rules_amount_modes`, `eurob2b_rules_form_rows`, `eurob2b_rules_action_from_request`, `eurob2b_rules_page_descriptions`, `eurob2b_price_tier_quantity`, `eurob2b_methods_page_notices`, `eurob2b_methods_page_sections`, `eurob2b_methods_page_save`, `eurob2b_vat_validation_enabled`, `eurob2b_vat_qualified_confirmation`, `eurob2b_vat_trader_details`, `eurob2b_tax_special_treatments`, `eurob2b_tax_treatment_input`, `eurob2b_tax_special_invoice_text`, `eurob2b_processing_record_vat_parts`, `eurob2b_processing_record_measures`, `eurob2b_processing_record_transfers`, `eurob2b_privacy_policy_vat_services`, `eurob2b_privacy_policy_intro`; interfaces `Rules\ConditionChecker`, `Tax\Treatment\SpecialTreatment`, `Migration\PriceTarget`; `Settings::stored()`; `TaxTreatmentDecider::combine()` takes the treatment input instead of a flag.
- Developers: `eurob2b_einvoice_data()` is defined by EuroB2B Pro. New filters `eurob2b_settings_transfer_parts`, `eurob2b_settings_transfer_warnings`, `eurob2b_audit_value_label`, `eurob2b_privacy_policy_sections` and `eurob2b_protected_forms` (docs/hooks.md). `ModuleRegistry::add()` takes the id of the module to insert after.

### Fixed
- Free edition (PLT-015): a group with its own currency (CUR-001, a Pro feature) lost its base prices without EuroB2B Pro, so its customers saw "Price on request". Without Pro every group now pays in the shop currency; the group currency field is shown only with Pro, a currency stored while Pro was active is kept and applies again when Pro is active (`Currency::group_currencies_enabled()`, filter `eurob2b_group_currencies_enabled`).

## [1.3.0] – 2026-10-29

The second EU package: tools for the tax audit, the tax adviser and late payers, an OSS check, the Peppol check of e-invoicing addresses and five more languages. Everything that sends, looks up or changes something is off by default or happens only by hand (export evidence, Peppol Directory lookup, dunning e-mails, DATEV export without a consultant number); the audit files and the OSS monitor only read what EuroB2B already recorded. No database migration (database version stays 12); new order item meta `_eurob2b_supply_type` records goods or service per line at checkout. The DATEV export follows the published format description and ships no accounts or tax keys: they come from the shop's tax adviser. The new translations are machine-assisted and not yet reviewed by native speakers.

### Added
- Translations into French (`fr_FR`), Italian (`it_IT`), Spanish (`es_ES`), Dutch (`nl_NL`) and Polish (`pl_PL`) (PLT-014): every string of PHP, the block editor JavaScript and block.json, with the plural forms of each language. They were made with machine assistance and a fixed glossary per language (WooCommerce's terms and form of address of each locale; legal citations of German and EU law stay as they are) and are not yet reviewed by native speakers; corrections are welcome. CI checks every catalogue for missing strings and matching placeholders like the German ones.
- Accounting export in the DATEV format (RPT-006): *EuroB2B → Accounting export* writes the revenue bookings of a month or a quarter as a booking batch file (EXTF, following the published DATEV format specification, booking batch version 13, header version 700) for the shop's tax adviser. EuroB2B chooses no account and no tax key: the shop fills in a mapping table with its tax adviser, one row per tax treatment recorded on the order (domestic, intra-EU exempt, other member state taxed, export, special territory, Switzerland, …), type of supply and tax rate, with the revenue account and the tax key (empty for automatic accounts). Sales without a row are listed with their orders and no file is created until they have one. The supply type (goods or service) is now kept on each order line at checkout (classic and Checkout block), so a later change or deletion of a product does not change how an order is booked; a line of a deleted product without it is never taken for goods. One booking per order, treatment, supply and rate with the gross amount, exact to the cent and matched to the invoice total; refunds as separate credit bookings in the period of the refund; the invoice number and date of PDF Invoices & Packing Slips (new filter `eurob2b_invoice_number`) else the order number and date; the VAT ID on exempt intra-EU bookings; no names or addresses in the file. Orders in other currencies are listed and left out. New settings *Settings → Accounting export (DATEV format)* (consultant number, client number, start of the fiscal year, length of the G/L accounts, chart of accounts, collective debtor account; all empty by default) and a debtor account per customer on the user profile (`_eurob2b_datev_debtor`, privacy export and erasure, data inventory D-31). The batch is not locked on import. Open questions for the tax adviser are listed in `docs/country-matrix.md` §19; the record of processing activities gets an activity EB-14 (wording approved by the product owner on 2026-10-28).
- Peppol Directory lookup (EIN-006): with the new setting *Peppol Directory lookup* (*Settings → E-invoicing*, off by default) EuroB2B looks a business customer's Peppol ID up in the public Peppol Directory of OpenPeppol AISBL when the ID is saved new or changed in My Account or on the user profile, and when staff click *Check again* on the user profile. Only the Peppol ID is sent; never in bulk, by schedule or at checkout; timeout 5 s, and a failed or slow lookup never blocks saving. The user profile shows *Listed* with the registered name and country, *Not listed* or *Lookup failed*, with the checked ID and the date, and says that "not listed" does not prove the ID is not registered in the Peppol network. The result is stored with the customer (`_eurob2b_peppol_lookup`, privacy D-30), removed when the ID changes or is removed, listed in the privacy export and erased with the e-invoicing data. Background: B2B e-invoices through Peppol are mandatory in Belgium since 2026-01-01 (`docs/einvoicing.md` §1 and §7). **The privacy policy section and the processing record lines await the product owner's approval.**
- Dunning of overdue business orders (PAY-023): *EuroB2B → Overdue orders* shows the dunning level of each order, filters by it and prepares a payment reminder, a first or a final dunning letter for one order or for selected rows; the order screen shows the history and the next step. Every e-mail goes out only after a preview (recipient, level, amounts, text) and a confirmation; nothing runs automatically. The levels go in order, each once, with *Settings → Payments → Days between dunning levels* (default 14) between them. The dunning letters state the default interest so far and the EUR 40 flat fee where the overdue list computes them, with their legal basis; the order itself is not changed. Three new WooCommerce e-mails (*EuroB2B: payment reminder*, *first dunning letter*, *final dunning letter*) with theme-overridable templates. History on the order (`_eurob2b_dunning`, privacy D-28) and an order note per e-mail; hook `eurob2b_dunning_sent`. **The e-mail texts await the product owner's approval.**
- Export evidence (Ausfuhrnachweis, TAX-032): with the new setting *Export evidence* (*Tax and VAT IDs*, off by default) the shop tracks, for every order whose goods EuroB2B treated as an export to a third country (Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Norway, the United Kingdom and other countries outside the EU, and special territories), the evidence that the goods left the EU (§ 6 UStG, §§ 8 to 11 UStDV). Shop staff record the type of evidence (electronic exit notice from ATLAS, carrier or courier document, other), the MRN of the export declaration (format check) and the date of exit, upload PDF, JPG or PNG documents into the private file storage, accept and reopen the evidence or mark it as not needed with a reason. The status shows in the order box; *EuroB2B → Proof of supply* gets a tab *Exports* with the orders by status and completion month, counts, days since completion and a check of earlier orders. Every change is in the audit log. Kept like the proof of supply (10 years from the end of the year of the latest entry), listed in the privacy export and kept on erasure requests (data inventory D-29). A missing evidence never changes the tax of an order. Sources in `docs/country-matrix.md` §18; the MRN check character is not verified (algorithm UNVERIFIED).
- Audit file (TAX-031): *Download audit file (PDF)* below the EuroB2B box of the order screen compiles for one order what EuroB2B recorded about its tax: order header and totals, the VAT ID with every validation record (service, time, consultation number, returned name and address, manual decisions), the tax treatment with its reason, the country-matrix rules behind it and their review status, the proof of intra-Community supply with its history, the e-invoicing data copied to the order, the terms page shown at checkout and the audit log entries. Missing data is printed as "not recorded". *EuroB2B → Audit files* builds a ZIP for a date range (by default only orders with an exemption: reverse charge, mixed, export) with one PDF per order and `summary.csv`, at most 100 orders per file; the file is deleted on the server right after the download. Needs the capability to decide on VAT ID checks. The file makes no statement on legal sufficiency. Developers add sections with the filter `eurob2b_audit_file_sections`.
- OSS threshold monitor (TAX-033): *EuroB2B → VAT IDs* shows, for the current and the previous calendar year, the net distance sales of goods to consumers in other EU member states against the EUR 10,000 threshold of Art. 59c VAT Directive and § 3c (4) UStG ("8,412.50 € of 10,000 €, 84 %"). Counted: orders in processing, completed and on hold, sent from the store address's member state to another member state and not exempted as an intra-Community B2B supply; Monaco counts as France, Northern Ireland (XI, or GB with a BT postcode) counts for goods, special territories outside the EU VAT area and pick-ups do not; refunds with item lines are subtracted. Service lines, orders in other currencies (not converted) and refunds without item lines are listed separately. The screen says plainly that the threshold covers the whole business across all sales channels and that telecommunications, broadcasting and electronic services also count but cannot be told apart. The totals are cached for a day, with a *Recalculate now* button. An admin notice appears at 80 % in the current year and when the threshold is exceeded in the current or the previous year; it can be hidden per year and level. With One Stop Shop for WooCommerce or Germanized active, the section points to their reports. Display only: nothing changes tax rates, tax classes or the treatment (`docs/country-matrix.md` §17).

## [1.2.0] – 2026-10-28

Four features for B2B quotes, payments and pricing. Existing shops behave as before until a setting, a rule or an offer line uses them; the only visible change is the *Download PDF* link next to *Print view* on offers. Database: migration 12 (nullable column `linked_product_id` in `eurob2b_quote_items`). The release archive now ships dompdf under its own namespace (`vendor-prefixed/`), so it does not collide with other plugins that bring dompdf.

### Added
- Offer PDF (OFR-011): *Download PDF* next to *Print view* in My Account and on the admin offer page gives the offer as a PDF file `offer-<id>.pdf` with logo, shop and customer address, lines, net total and a footer with the page number on every page. Same access rules as the print view, a signed link per offer. *Settings → Quotes and offers → Attach the offer as PDF* (off by default) attaches it to the offer e-mail; the file is deleted after sending. The template `offer-pdf.php` can be overridden from the theme; developers add header and footer content (`eurob2b_offer_pdf_header`, `eurob2b_offer_pdf_footer`) and fonts for other scripts (`eurob2b_offer_pdf_fonts`, `eurob2b_offer_pdf_font_family`; DejaVu Sans covers Latin, Greek and Cyrillic). Rendered on the server with the bundled library dompdf 3.1.6 (LGPL-2.1), without loading anything from the network. EuroB2B ships its own copy under a separate namespace, so it never clashes with the dompdf of PDF invoice plugins such as PDF Invoices & Packing Slips (WP Overnight); the release archive grows by about 4.2 MB, mostly fonts (docs/architecture.md §13).
- Free-text offer lines linked to a product for stock (OFR-002): in the offer editor a free-text line can name a product or variation under *Linked product for stock*. The customer still sees only the description and the price, and the line stays a fee on the order (same tax and invoice). When the order reduces stock, the linked product's stock goes down by the line's quantity: for business orders from the business stock when the product keeps one, not at all with *Business orders reduce no stock*. A linked line at 0.00 (a part included in another line's price) comes as a fee of 0.00 so it moves stock too; unlinked lines at 0.00 stay out as before. Cancellation gives it back; a refund with *Restock refunded items* gives back the share the refunded amount covers, in whole units and at the precision of the order currency. The cart and both checkouts refuse a linked line when its product manages stock, allows no backorders and has too little stock (for business customers the business stock, with *Always in stock for business customers* backorders are allowed), naming the line and the available quantity. The order screen shows the link on the fee. Unlinked free-text lines behave as before. Database: migration 12 adds the nullable column `linked_product_id` to `eurob2b_quote_items`.
- Purchase order document (PAY-015): *Settings → Payments → Purchase order document* (off by default, optional or required) lets customers who order with the purchase order gateway hand in their purchase order as a PDF, JPG or PNG file. In the classic checkout the file is uploaded at checkout, and "Required" refuses the order without one. The Checkout block has no file upload: the order is placed, marked "Purchase order document missing" when the document is required, and the customer uploads it on the order page in My Account or on the thank-you page. The order screen shows the status, lists the documents with a download link and lets shop staff add or replace one. The files stay in the private file storage with the order; only staff who may edit orders and the order's own customer can download them. They are deleted with the order or when WooCommerce anonymises it, listed in the privacy export and kept on erasure requests (data inventory D-25). While the setting is on, the privacy policy text suggestion has a section on purchase order documents and the record of processing activities lists them (texts approved by the product owner).
- Discount rules as a line in the cart (PRC-018): per discount rule, *Show as* → *Discount line in the cart* keeps the product's price on product pages and in the cart lines and shows the discount as a named, non-removable line in the cart, the checkout, the Cart and Checkout blocks and the order. The line is a WooCommerce coupon of its own type, so VAT is reduced per tax rate of the discounted products (no negative fee); the same rules decide as in the price mode (audience, scope, priority, stacking). The default stays *Lower price (sale price with label)*; existing rules are unchanged.

## [1.1.0] – 2026-10-28

The EU package after 1.0 and a mode for business-only shops. New tax and legal behaviour is off by default where it changes existing shops (special territories, withdrawal button); Monaco is treated as France from this version on. Points marked UNVERIFIED in `docs/country-matrix.md` (ZM rounding, day count of default interest, the account confirmation of receipt) wait for a tax adviser's confirmation.

### Added
- Business customers only (ACC-009): *Settings → Guest access → Business customers only* (off by default) closes the shop to consumers. Only approved business customers see prices and can buy; private customers who already have an account and customers without a customer type are treated like guests (prices hidden, no cart, no checkout in the classic checkout and the Checkout block) and read their own text; guests get at least *Hide prices*; the registration offers business types only. Shop staff are not affected.
- Legal texts per customer type (EU package, LGL-001, LGL-002): *EuroB2B → Settings → Legal texts* takes a terms and conditions page for business customers. Approved business customers see it wherever the shop links the terms (checkout, Checkout block, Germanized's legal checkboxes), and the order records which page and version applied. *Withdrawal function only for consumers* (off by default) withholds the withdrawal button of "EU Order Withdrawal Button" (also in Germanized) from orders of approved business customers (§ 356a BGB applies to consumers; how mixed shops may handle it is not officially settled, see `docs/country-matrix.md` §15).
- Proof of intra-Community supply (Gelangensnachweis, TAX-030; the account confirmation is an electronic procedure in the sense of UStAE 6a.4 Abs. 3, agreed with the customer, e.g. in the business terms; EU package, product owner decision of 2026-10-28): for every order whose goods EuroB2B exempted as an intra-Community supply, the shop requests, stores and tracks the proof that the goods reached another member state under § 17b UStDV. It never changes the tax of an order (CJEU C-639/24).
  - The customer confirms place and month of receipt in My Account (order page and the tab *Confirm receipt*): only the order's own customer, once, as a stored snapshot with buyer, goods, place, month, date and confirming account, in German or English.
  - *EuroB2B → Proof of supply*: orders by status and completion month with counts and days since completion; per order the request e-mail (*EuroB2B: confirmation of receipt requested*; guests are asked for a signed confirmation), document upload into the private file storage (signed confirmation, CMR, forwarder certificate, courier tracking, other), accept and reopen, and a printable view. The status shows in the order box, and the VAT IDs page warns about completed orders without a proof.
  - Kept 10 years from the end of the year of the latest entry (setting *Keep validation evidence linked to orders*), then deleted by the retention job; listed in the privacy export and kept on erasure requests (data inventory D-23, docs/tax.md, country-matrix §13).
- Recapitulative statement upload file for Germany (TAX-029, EU package): *EuroB2B → VAT IDs* builds, for a month or quarter, the file the ZM online form of ELSTER and the BZSt Online-Portal imports (CSV version 3.0): one line per customer VAT ID and type of supply (goods `L`, services `S`), whole euros (the cents of each line's sum are cut toward zero, as common ZM software does; ZM-04), at most 1,500 lines per file. Goods count by their invoice date (PDF Invoices & Packing Slips, or the new filter `eurob2b_recapitulative_invoice_date`), services by the completion date; refunds reduce the period they were made in. The preview lists what is not in the file: orders without an invoice date, refunds without item lines and orders in another currency. Sources in `docs/country-matrix.md` §12.
- Special territories (TAX-024): with the new setting *Special territories* (*Tax and VAT IDs*, off by default) addresses with a member state's country code that lie outside the EU VAT area are treated like a third country. Covered: Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla (Spanish state, postcode as fallback), the French overseas territories (postcodes 97xxx, 986xx, 987xx), Heligoland (27498), Büsingen (78266), Campione d'Italia (22061) and Livigno (23041). The order becomes an export with the reason *Territory outside the EU VAT area* for business customers and consumers, WooCommerce applies only the rates set up for all countries, and the order stores the territory, shows it on the order screen and names it in the invoice text. Monaco (FR 98000) stays France. Every rule carries its sources (checked 2026-10-28) and is listed on the VAT IDs page; UNVERIFIED rules (Mount Athos, Åland by Finnish postcode, Heligoland 27486, Livigno 23030) are never applied (`docs/country-matrix.md` §8).
- Overdue B2B orders (PAY-021): *EuroB2B → Overdue orders* lists unpaid orders of business customers past their due date with days overdue, open amount, default interest so far (base rate + 9 percentage points per half-year, § 288 (2) BGB, on the actual days of the calendar year: 366 in a leap year; the day count is not officially fixed, LP-07), the EUR 40 flat fee for orders in euro (§ 288 (5) BGB) and the total claim, with a CSV download. The EuroB2B box of an overdue order shows days overdue and interest. A calculation aid only; nothing is charged. The base rates of the Deutsche Bundesbank ship with the plugin; newer half-years can be added under *Settings → Payments → Newer base rates*, and the screen warns when the current half-year is missing.
- Payment terms over 60 days (PAY-022): business groups with longer terms get a warning on *Payment & shipping* (§ 271a BGB; 30 days for public authorities in the help text). Saving stays possible.
- Privacy policy text suggestion: a section on the confirmation of receipt for deliveries to another EU country (TAX-030, data inventory D-23), shown while the tax exemption is on.

### Fixed
- Monaco is treated as France for VAT (Art. 7 (1) VAT Directive, product owner decision of 2026-10-28). Before, a Monaco address counted as a third country, so orders there were treated as exports: consumers in Monaco could be charged no VAT, and Monaco businesses with a French VAT ID never got the intra-Community treatment. The shop needs WooCommerce tax rates for the country Monaco (the French ones); Monaco can now be chosen for the VAT ID field.

## [1.0.0] – 2026-10-28

First release. It covers every requirement planned for 1.0 in `docs/feature-matrix.md` and the packages built before the tag; the entries below list the changes since development began. The tax rules of the country profiles are sourced but not yet signed off by a tax professional (`docs/country-matrix.md`).

### Changed
- Storefront UI: all customer-facing forms and tables follow the theme (docs/frontend.md). This covers the order form, CSV quick order, purchase lists, quotes and offers, the company and approvals tabs, the tier table, the quote control and the cart buttons.
  - They use WooCommerce's and WordPress's own classes, so the theme draws buttons, fields and tables like the rest of the shop.
  - One shared stylesheet with zero-specificity defaults and design tokens replaces `order-form.css`.
  - Tables become labelled rows on small screens; the order form turns each line into a block.
  - The order form follows the width the theme gives it, not the window (CSS container query). In My Account and other narrow columns the lines stack instead of scrolling sideways, and stacked lines label their fields (SKU, quantity, stock, unit price, total).
  - Primary buttons carry WooCommerce's primary class `button alt`, so classic themes such as Storefront draw them as the shop's main button instead of a plain one.
  - On phones, action bars stack their buttons at full width with the main action on top, and the action cell of a stacked table drops its label.
  - In the classic cart, "Save as purchase list" and "Request a quote for this cart" each get a line of their own. The list name field was only 80px wide: WooCommerce's rule for the coupon field applied to it. The quote button sat outside EuroB2B's styles, so in themes without button styles it showed the browser's plain button.
- The offer print view has calmer typography.
- The My Account tabs *Order form* and *CSV upload* are on by default (product owner decision of 2026-10-22). Business customers find both features without a page holding the shortcode or block. The tabs follow the order form audience (default: business customers), and a shop that saved the setting keeps its stored value.
- The German translation `de_DE` addresses the reader with "du", as WordPress `de_DE` does; `de_DE_formal` keeps "Sie" (product owner decision of 2026-10-20). 195 texts changed; shops that want "Sie" choose *Deutsch (Sie)* as the site language. A unit test keeps each catalog to its form of address.

### Added

- `SECURITY.md`: security problems are reported by e-mail to info@storetown-media.de (finding P3-1 of the review before 1.0, product owner decision of 2026-10-27).
- Guides for shop owners under `docs/guide/` (product owner decision of 2026-10-20: before the 1.0 tag), with the German admin labels in brackets: the setup guide (requirements, groups, registration, guest access, prices and rules, companies, ordering, quotes, payment and shipping, classic checkout vs. Checkout block, language), the tax guide (VAT ID checks, evidence and retention, the treatment table, exemption prerequisites, invoice texts, reports; marked as not tax advice, with the UNVERIFIED points of the country matrix) and the privacy guide (stored data, external connections, privacy policy text, processing record, export and erasure, retention settings). The README points to them.
- Connection to Germanized Pro (EIN-005, part 2; product owner decision of 2026-10-25, plugin provided on 2026-10-27): while Germanized Pro is active, its e-invoices (ZUGFeRD, XRechnung 1 to 3, Peppol BIS 3.0; written against 4.3.2) get EuroB2B's buyer reference (the plugin's routing id), purchase order number, company register entry and Leitweg-ID or Peppol ID as electronic address, where the plugin's form was left empty or held its own stand-ins ("NA", the order number, the buyer's e-mail address).
  - For orders EuroB2B exempted, the VAT category, VATEX code and exemption text follow EuroB2B's reason, as for PDF Invoices & Packing Slips; the plugin alone decided between reverse charge and intra-Community supply by whether all products are virtual. The small business category, other reasons and mixed orders stay with the plugin.
  - XRechnung created by hand still asks for the routing ID in the plugin's form. See `docs/einvoicing.md` §6.
  - Checked on the test shop with the files the plugin created (ZUGFeRD Extended, XRechnung 3, Peppol BIS); Germanized Pro 4.3.2 supports Germanized up to 4.0, so the check ran with Germanized 4.0.11 (`docs/einvoicing.md` §6.6). EIN-005 is done.
- Switzerland, Liechtenstein and Norway (CTY-005; product owner decisions of 2026-10-25 and 2026-10-27):
  - Two new checks, each off by default with its own setting under *Tax and VAT IDs*: Swiss VAT numbers (CHE) in the UID register of the Swiss Federal Statistical Office (`ValidateVatNumber`, valid only while the VAT number is active) and Norwegian organisation numbers in the Brønnøysund Unit Register (in the VAT register and not deleted). Only the number is sent; for a number Brønnøysund confirms, the company name and business address it returned are kept as evidence. Both use the existing evidence storage, retries, downtime policy, review page and VAT ID field.
  - Liechtenstein: the five-digit Liechtenstein VAT number is accepted (`LI12345`, or `12345` with the billing country LI) and checked for its format only; Liechtenstein businesses with a UID may enter their CHE number.
  - CHE and Norwegian numbers must have the right check digit, so typing errors are caught before anything is sent.
  - The country profiles CH, LI and NO carry their VAT rates and registration thresholds with sources (rules version 2026.10.2); the VAT IDs page lists them. The tax decision is unchanged: EuroB2B never exempts customers in these countries, WooCommerce rates decide.
  - CH, LI and NO can be chosen as countries whose VAT IDs are checked at checkout; they are not in the default list.
  - The privacy-policy suggestion and the record of processing activities name the two services while they are on, including the transfer to Switzerland under its adequacy decision (texts approved on 2026-10-27).
- Import from Wholesale Suite (MIG-003): the same section takes over Wholesale Prices' wholesale roles as business customer groups, the customers with these roles and the wholesale prices of products and variations (a price entered as a percentage as the stored price; a price above the regular price with a warning). The general discount per role is imported switched off, because Wholesale Suite gives it only on products without a wholesale price. Data of the paid add-ons is not imported. See `docs/migration.md`.
- Import from B2BKing (MIG-001, MIG-002; product owner decision of 2026-10-25): *EuroB2B → Transfer settings → Import from another plugin* takes over a B2BKing shop's customer groups, customers (type, group, approval status, registration type and field values), registration roles and fields, sub-accounts as company accounts, group and customer prices with tiers and quantity limits, payment and shipping methods, rules and product visibility.
  - B2BKing's data is only read, never changed; it works also when B2BKing is already deactivated.
  - A test run shows first what would be new, changed or not imported, and why. The import writes through EuroB2B's own screens' validation and keeps every value it replaces (migration 11, table `eurob2b_migration_log`), so it can be undone for 30 days or until it is finished. A second import changes nothing.
  - Not imported: tax exemptions, VAT IDs and tax rules (the tax module decides on VAT), offers, conversations, purchase lists and uploaded files. Rules EuroB2B cannot express exactly are imported switched off with a warning.
  - Big shops: batches in the background, and `wp eurob2b import`. See `docs/migration.md`.
- FOX – Currency Switcher as the one supported currency switcher (CUR-002, CUR-003; product owner decision of 2026-10-25), an optional integration. EuroB2B resolves group, customer, rule and tier prices in the shop currency and FOX converts the result. When customers pay in the currency FOX shows, EuroB2B converts its own thresholds, fixed fees, fixed payment surcharges, coupon values per group, free-text lines of accepted offers and the RRP with FOX's rate (exactly, rounded once), the tier table and the order form follow FOX, and the checkout re-validation compares with the price converted the way FOX converts the cart line. Customers of a group with its own currency are never switched or converted by FOX. Quote requests and offers stay in the currency of the customer's prices. Tested in CI with FOX 1.5.5.
- Currency per customer group (CUR-001, CUR-003; product owner decision of 2026-10-25): a group can pay in a currency of its own (EuroB2B → Groups, REST `currency`), without conversion. Its customers see and pay in that currency on the storefront and in both checkouts, and the order is stored in it. Group prices, group tier sets and customer prices are entered per currency on the product and variation screen, in the price lists and through the REST API; they are stored under the existing meta keys with the currency appended (`_eurob2b_group_price_7_chf`), so a change of currency never reads old prices in the new one. The shop's own prices do not apply to such a group: a product without a price in its currency shows "Price on request" and cannot be bought. Rules get a *Currency of the amounts*: rules with prices, fixed amounts or a cart value apply only to customers who pay in their currency, rules with percentages or quantities everywhere. Fixed payment surcharges stay in the shop currency, coupons with amounts in the shop currency and offers in another currency are refused with a message. New module `currency`.
- Connection to PDF Invoices & Packing Slips for WooCommerce (EIN-005, part 1; product owner decision of 2026-10-25): while the plugin is active, its e-documents (UBL 2.1, Peppol BIS 3.0, CII D16B, Factur-X, ZUGFeRD; tested with 5.16.6) carry EuroB2B's buyer reference, purchase order number, company register entry, Leitweg-ID or Peppol ID as electronic address and, when the plugin found none, the VAT ID.
  - For orders EuroB2B exempted, the VAT category and exemption code follow EuroB2B's reason: intra-Community supply of goods K with VATEX-EU-IC, reverse-charge services AE with VATEX-EU-AE (VATEX code list 9.0), on every line and in the tax breakdown, with EuroB2B's exemption text. Without settings the plugin used reverse charge for every exempt order. Other reasons and mixed orders keep the plugin's classification; amounts and tax never change.
  - In CII, EuroB2B adds the delivery country and date that category K needs and writes the exemption text in the order the CII schema expects.
  - Only the plugin's public filters are used; the filter `eurob2b_einvoice_connection` switches the connection off, `eurob2b_einvoice_vat_category` changes the mapping. The plugin creates no XRechnung. Germanized Pro follows. See `docs/einvoicing.md` §5.
- Documents with the registration (REG-016, PRV-006; product owner decision of 2026-10-25): the field type *File* in the registration form takes a PDF, JPG or PNG up to 5 MB (setting). Files are checked by content, kept in a private folder under random names (migration 10, table `eurob2b_files`; `EUROB2B_PRIVATE_DIR` moves the folder outside the web root) and downloaded only by staff who manage customers, from the approval card. They are deleted 30 days after the approval or rejection (setting), with the account and on an erasure request. A Site Health test reports when the folder can be reached from the web (nginx ignores `.htaccess`). File fields never appear at checkout, on the account page or in quote forms.
- Quote requests from guests (ACC-006, QUO-015, off by default; product owner decision of 2026-10-25): visitors without a customer account collect products and send a request with name, e-mail address and, optionally, company on the page with the quote form. The shop replies by e-mail and, once the guest has a customer account, assigns the request to it to make an offer. Honeypot, captcha hook and at most 3 requests per connection and hour; the confirmation e-mail carries no text the guest typed; guest requests are deleted 180 days after their last change. Migration 9 adds the guest columns to the quote table. The suggested privacy policy text names the IP-based abuse protection of the registration, guest requests and the order form. See `docs/quotes.md`.
- Separate stock for business customers (STK-003, STK-004, STK-005, STK-007, off by default): products and variations can keep a second stock for business customers, switched on per product in the inventory tab.
  - Business customers see and buy from that stock, with its own backorder setting (or its own stock status without stock management); everybody else uses WooCommerce's stock.
  - Orders recorded as business orders reduce it and get it back on cancellation, on refunds with restock and on item edits in the admin; the Checkout block holds no stock for them.
  - Low and out-of-stock e-mails for the business stock follow WooCommerce's notification settings. See `docs/stock.md`.
- Log in as a member (CMP-011, off by default): with *Log in as a member* switched on, the owner of a company account can act in the account of an administrator or member, a company administrator in the account of a member, for 30 minutes, e.g. to help with an order.
  - *My Account → Company* offers *Log in as* next to these members. Never into shop staff accounts, accounts with other roles, other companies or accounts that are not approved.
  - A strip at the top of every page shows whose account is in use and until when, with *Back to my account*. The account details cannot be changed meanwhile.
  - The audit log records the start, the end and every order placed meanwhile. See `docs/companies.md`.
- Audit log of administrative decisions (PRV-011): EuroB2B → *Audit log* lists who approved or rejected registrations, changed a customer's status, type or group, decided a VAT ID check manually, or approved or rejected an order waiting for approval, and when.
  - One entry per changed value, with user IDs, the order or check number and the previous and new value. No IP addresses, notes or names; names are looked up when shown.
  - Entries are deleted after 365 days by the daily retention job (setting under Settings → Privacy and data, 30 to 3650 days).
  - An erasure request or the deletion of an account removes the person from the entries; the entries stay. The privacy export lists a person's entries without the other person.
  - Read only, for users who manage EuroB2B's settings. New table `eurob2b_audit_log` (migration 8).
- Record of processing activities (PRV-010): EuroB2B → *Processing record* shows a draft for the shop owner's record under Art. 30 GDPR, laid out like the template of the German data protection authorities.
  - One activity per purpose: registration and approval, individual conditions, VAT ID checks, company accounts, order details, quotes, purchase lists, imports, abuse protection, conversations, sales reports and e-invoicing data. Each lists purposes, data subjects, data, persons with access, external recipients, erasure periods and legal-basis candidates.
  - Erasure periods, external recipients (VIES, BZSt) and switched-off features follow the current settings. The controller sheet takes the site title and the WooCommerce store address and marks what the shop owner must add.
  - Print view (also for saving as PDF) and a download as an HTML file to edit in a word processor. Nothing is stored.
  - Extensions add their own activities with the filter `eurob2b_processing_activities`.
- Privacy self-test (PRV-009, EU package).
  - Site Health and the screens *Export/Erase Personal Data* warn when a plugin breaks WordPress's privacy tools by replacing the list of exporters or erasers instead of extending it.
  - The warning names the plugin and the callback, and lists the data that would silently be left out of requests.
  - Seen with Germanized 4.1.5 and its EU order withdrawal button package.
- Company register entry (CTY-006).
  - Business customers and staff store register, number and court with the e-invoicing data.
  - German registers (HRA, HRB, GnR, PR, VR, GsR) need the court; the Austrian Firmenbuch number needs its check letter; other registers are kept as given.
  - The entry goes on the order and is handed to invoice plugins as BT-47.
- E-invoicing data (EIN-001 … EIN-004, first part of the EU package).
  - Business customers store their reference on invoices, Leitweg-ID and Peppol ID under My Account. The Leitweg-ID check digit is verified after the KoSIT specification.
  - An optional checkout field "Your reference" (classic checkout and Checkout block) sets the order's buyer reference.
  - The values go on the order, the order screen and the e-mails.
  - `eurob2b_einvoice_data( $order )` hands them to invoice plugins with the terms of EN 16931 (BT-10, BT-13, BT-48, BT-49, BT-120) plus EuroB2B's exemption reason code.
  - Covered by privacy export and erasure (also the copies on orders), and by order anonymisation.
  - EuroB2B still creates no invoices. See `docs/einvoicing.md`.
- REST API `eurob2b/v1`: prices (API-004).
  - `/products/<id>/prices` reads and changes the group prices, customer prices and tier sets of a product or variation.
  - `/prices/batch` changes up to 100 products, answered product by product.
  - Prices are stored by the classes the product screen uses, so they read back the same there.
  - Invalid prices or tiers are refused with every message, and nothing of that product is written.
  - Group and customer prices each need their capability, and every product needs the right to edit it.
  - See `docs/rest-api.md`.
- REST API `eurob2b/v1`: company accounts (CMP-013).
  - `/companies` lists, creates, reads, changes and deletes company accounts.
  - `/companies/<id>/members` links existing users, changes their permissions and approval settings, and unlinks them, through the same service as the Companies page.
  - Besides `eurob2b_manage_companies`, every change that reclassifies a user account needs the right to edit that account.
  - Addresses and e-mail addresses are not part of the API.
  - See `docs/rest-api.md`.
- REST API `eurob2b/v1`, part 2 (API-001, issue #62):
  - Rules: list (filter by type, switch and audience token), create, read, change, delete, and a batch route for up to 100 lines answered line by line, e.g. one fixed price per customer (PRC-008). The API accepts exactly what the Rules page accepts, with the page's messages; audience tokens must name existing groups and users.
  - VAT ID validations: list (by customer, order, result, country), read, and the manual decision of the VAT IDs page. Trader names and addresses returned by VIES or BZSt are not part of the API; no route starts an online check.
  - See `docs/rest-api.md`.
- Registration through form builders (REG-011): `eurob2b_register_business_customer( $user_id, $data )`, as a function and as an action, turns a user created by a form builder into a business customer.
  - It stores the registration type, custom field values, terms acceptance and VAT ID with the validation of EuroB2B's own form.
  - The customer is pending or approved as the registration type says, and the usual e-mails go out.
  - `eurob2b_validate_business_registration( $data )` checks the data in the form builder's validation step, before the user exists.
  - Staff accounts and existing customers are refused.
  - See `docs/form-builders.md`.
- Transfer settings (PLT-012): *EuroB2B → Transfer settings* downloads the configuration as a JSON file and imports such a file into another shop or back into the same one.
  - The file holds the settings, customer groups, registration types and fields, quote request fields, payment and shipping methods per audience, payment terms, and the surcharges, discounts and shipping costs. Rules, prices, customers, companies, quotes and orders are not part of it, nor is the setting that deletes the data on uninstall.
  - The import has two steps: *Check file* shows what would change, *Import now* writes it. Groups are matched by slug (default groups by their role), missing groups are created and every group reference is adjusted to the local ids. References to single customers, companies or pages are flagged.
  - Before the import the current configuration is kept as a backup that can be downloaded and imported to go back.
  - See `docs/settings-transfer.md`.
- Multisite (PLT-012): a network activation provisions every site, a site added to the network is provisioned when EuroB2B is network-active, and the uninstall follows each site's own data deletion setting. Customer values stay shared between the sites of a network because WordPress keeps user meta once per network; the limitation is documented. A CI job runs the multisite tests.
- Conversations (MSG-001…007, off by default): customers write to the shop under *My Account → Messages* (message or inquiry, optionally about one of their orders) and read the replies there; the shop answers and starts conversations under *EuroB2B → Conversations* with an unread counter and a dashboard widget. E-mails both ways as WooCommerce e-mail types. Members of a company account with *View company conversations* read and answer the company's conversations. Export, erasure and a retention of 24 months after the last message (product owner decision of 2026-10-23); no attachments. Migration 7 creates the tables `eurob2b_conversations` and `eurob2b_messages`. See `docs/conversations.md`.
- Coupon values per group (CPN-003, opt-in): with *Coupon values per group* switched on, a coupon gets a value per customer group, so the same code gives each group its own discount in the cart, the classic checkout and the blocks. See `docs/pricing.md`.
- Recommended retail price (PRC-009, off by default): business customers (or another audience) see "RRP …" under the price when it is above the price shown; the source is the product's regular price or an own RRP field per product and variation. See `docs/pricing.md`.
- Free shipping rule (RUL-014) and shipping costs per group (SHP-004): the rule type *Free shipping* adds a shipping rate at no cost for its audience and conditions (optionally hiding the other rates except local pickup); a table on *Payment & shipping* changes the shipping cost per group or for guests by a percentage or an amount, as a real shipping line with its tax. See `docs/payments.md`.
- Surcharges and discounts per payment method (PAY-018/PAY-019, product owner decision of 2026-10-23): a fee line for the chosen method, a percentage of goods plus shipping (net) or a fixed amount, taxed at the standard rate, updated on every change of the method in the classic checkout and the Checkout block. Discounts for everyone; surcharges for approved business customers only and only on methods the shop owner releases; SEPA transfers and direct debits never carry one (§ 270a BGB). An order whose method would change the total the customer saw is refused in both checkouts. See `docs/payments.md`.
- Fee rules (RUL-013): the rule type *Fee* adds a named fee line to the cart and checkout (classic and blocks): a fixed amount per order, a percentage of the net value in scope or an amount per unit in scope, net, taxable with a chosen tax class (standard by default). Audience, scope, conditions and date window work as for every rule. A fee cannot depend on the payment method (payment surcharges are restricted by § 270a BGB and wait for their own decision). See `docs/rules.md`.
- Group upgrade suggestions (GRP-008): a group can name a target group, a net sales threshold and a period (last 12 months, previous year, previous month, all time). *EuroB2B → Group suggestions* lists the approved customers who reached it; staff move them by click (also all at once) or keep them in their group. Nothing changes automatically (product owner decision of 2026-10-23). See `docs/group-upgrades.md`.
- Stock for business customers (STK-001, STK-002, STK-006), *EuroB2B → Settings → Stock*:
  - *Always in stock for business customers*: they can order out-of-stock products as a backorder; everyone else still sees "out of stock".
  - *Business orders do not reduce stock*, decided by the customer type recorded on the order.
  - *Exact numbers only for business customers*: private customers and guests see "In stock" without the quantity, also in the Store API.
  - Separate B2B and B2C stock pools are deferred (product owner decision of 2026-10-23). See `docs/stock.md`.
- Single variations can be hidden per audience (VIS-009): a variation field *EuroB2B visibility* limits it to the ticked customer types or groups. For everyone else it is left out of the variation form, the Store API product and the price range, and it cannot be bought. A variation of a hidden product now also answers 404 in the Store API.
- Variations table on the product page (VAR-001…005): all variations of a variable product in one table with a quantity field per row, below WooCommerce's variation form or instead of it.
  - Switched on in the shop settings, per category or per product (*Advanced* tab); an audience setting limits it to some customers.
  - Variant, SKU, availability, unit price with the customer's tiers; with two or more attributes, filters per attribute; a compact block per variation on phones.
  - One click adds every row with a quantity through the cart's own checks; refused rows are reported.
  - The running total and the tier prices follow the quantities, counted per variation or across all variations of the product, as the tier quantity basis says.
  - See `docs/variations-table.md`.
- Price lists (PRC-022, issue #75): *EuroB2B → Price lists* exports the prices as a CSV for editing in Excel and imports the edited file.
  - The export has every product and variation (optionally one category) with the tier set for everyone and, per chosen group, the price, sale price and tier set; optionally one customer's prices.
  - Columns are named by the group slug or the customer's user id; tier sets read `10:9,50 | 50:8,90`; amounts use the shop's decimal separator.
  - The import finds products by ID, else SKU, checks every cell and reports problems per row. *Check file* is a test run that saves nothing. Empty cells keep the stored price unless the import is told to remove it.
  - Changes go through the same storage as the product screen; each changed product is saved once.
  - See `docs/pricing.md`.
- Reports (RPT-001…005, issue #62 part E): *EuroB2B → Reports* shows sales by day or month, customer type, group and company.
  - Filters: this month, last month, this year, last year or a custom period; all customers, business or private customers, one group, one company or one customer.
  - Metrics: orders, items, gross and net sales, average order value (net), refunds, coupon discounts, shipping and tax, added up exactly. Refunds count with the order they refund.
  - Customer type, group and company are the ones recorded on the order at purchase. Orders in another currency are left out and counted.
  - The counted order statuses are a setting (default: processing, completed, on hold).
  - Every table has a CSV download (Excel-friendly: BOM, semicolon by default, the shop's decimal separator).
  - See `docs/reports.md`.
- Accessibility review of the storefront (PLT-013, docs/accessibility.md): EuroB2B's customer-facing parts target WCAG 2.2 Level AA, as the BFSG / European Accessibility Act expects from shops that sell to consumers. 18 findings fixed:
  - The order form announces the number of search hits instead of reading the whole result list on every keystroke. The focus stays in place when rows are removed, more hits are loaded or the form is busy. An unknown SKU is marked on the field and announced. Network errors show the translated message.
  - The inline quote form shows the errors of a submission (they were not printed at all).
  - Controls repeated per row (quantity, remove, save, approve, delete) name their product, member, list or order. Help texts are linked to their fields, and classic checkout errors are linked to the VAT ID and purchase order fields.
  - Secondary text keeps the theme's text colour (a lighter mix fell below 4.5:1 with grey theme text). Focus outlines can no longer be removed by a theme's reset. Table headers stay readable on phones.
  - My Account headings start at h2. The tier table has a caption and marks the applying tier. The company address fields autocomplete. Deleting a list, emptying a quote request and removing a member ask first.
- Order form: "Import from a file or Excel" (ORD-013).
  - Customers paste rows copied in Excel or choose a CSV file; the rows fill the form, where they check quantities and prices before adding to the cart.
  - Rows are matched by SKU, or by exact product name when exactly one orderable product carries it. Ambiguous names and unavailable products are reported per row, never guessed.
  - A row without a quantity gets the smallest valid quantity.
  - The import uses the CSV quick order's format, size limit and switch, and can be turned off per form (block toggle, `import` shortcode attribute).
- Purchase lists: "Import from a file or Excel" on a list the customer owns, with the same technique. The rows are added to the list, and a product already on it gets the quantity added.
- CSV quick order: the same import technique as the order form.
  - Product names count when unique, and the quantity is optional.
  - Repeated rows are added up with a note.
  - Rows can be pasted from Excel instead of choosing a file.
  - Windows-1252 files from German Excel are read correctly.
  - Messages come in row order.
- REST API `eurob2b/v1`, part 1 (API-001, issue #62):
  - Groups: list, create, read, update and delete with the members moved to another group of the same customer type.
  - Customers: list with the filters of the users list, read, and change customer type, status and group.
  - The API is capability-protected and paginated (at most 100 per page). It returns EuroB2B's classification by user id only, never names or e-mail addresses, and writes through the same services as the admin screens.
  - See `docs/rest-api.md`.
- Customer prices (PRC-007): an own regular and sale price and tier set per customer and product or variation, stored as product meta (`_eurob2b_customer_price_<user id>`, `_eurob2b_customer_sale_price_<user id>`, `_eurob2b_tiers_user_<user id>`); they replace group prices and fixed-price rules for that customer, discount rules still apply on top; edited on the product screen for products and variations and on the customer's user profile, carried by WooCommerce's CSV export and import; exported and erased with the privacy tools and removed with the account. A fixed-price rule for one customer (`user:<id>`) is tested (PRC-008). See `docs/pricing.md`.
- Test of the CSV round trip for group prices: WooCommerce's exporter writes them as `Meta:` columns, its importer maps them back (PRC-003).

### Changed
- A valid VAT ID result older than *Re-check a valid number after* (default 30 days) no longer exempts the cart: the VAT ID field at checkout checks the number again and only the fresh result exempts; outages follow the downtime policy, manual decisions stay final (TAX-010, product owner decision of 2026-10-19). See `docs/tax.md`.
- 1.0 scope (product owner decision of 2026-10-19): RUL-011 tax exemption rule, PRC-018 discount as cart line, OFR-002 free-text line with stock sync, QUO-013 audit trail and TAX-012 bulk re-check queue move to later; ORD-003, GRP-006, GRP-007 and PRC-004 are done for 1.0 with their remaining parts documented.
- Product lists no longer cost one query per product: group prices, tiers, quantity limits and the supply type are read from the post meta cache WordPress primed for the list; enabled rules load with one query per request instead of one per rule type; category and product visibility rules load with one query per request (the category query returned every category because WooCommerce replaces its meta key). A 50-product shop page as a business customer went from 57 to 4 extra queries (PLT-011).

### Removed
- The setting *Shop mode* (`shop_mode`, *Hybrid* or *B2B only*) and with it the *General* settings tab (product owner decision of 2026-10-27). No code ever read the value, so *B2B only* changed nothing in the shop. A B2B-only shop is set up with guest access and the registration types; `docs/guide/setup.md` shows how. A real B2B-only mode is planned after 1.0 (ACC-009). A value a shop saved earlier stays in the option without effect and is dropped with the next save of the settings; a settings file from another shop that still holds it imports without it.
